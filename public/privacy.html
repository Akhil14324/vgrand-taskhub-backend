Privacy Policy

TaskHub — Multi-Business Task Monitoring

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Information We Collect

TaskHub collects the following information to provide and improve the service:

  • Account information: your name, username, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Profile and role data: your assigned role (user, admin, or super admin), business assignments, and account status.
  • Task and work data: tasks you create or are assigned, task status, due dates, descriptions, and attachments.
  • Communications: chat messages, attachments, and conversation metadata between users within your organization.
  • Notifications: push notification tokens delivered through Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) so we can send you task, chat, and warning alerts.
  • Usage data: device identifiers and limited app usage information used for security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve TaskHub features.
  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account.
  • Assign and track tasks, send warnings, and monitor completion across businesses.
  • Deliver chat messaging between authorized users.
  • Send push notifications about tasks, messages, and account activity.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

How We Share Your Information

TaskHub is a business task-management tool. Your account, task, and chat data is visible to the administrators and super administrators within your organization, as needed to manage work. We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:

  • With authorized administrators in your organization as part of normal app functionality.
  • With service providers (such as hosting and push notification providers) who process data on our behalf under appropriate agreements.
  • When required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of TaskHub, our users, or others.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account from within the app, your account and associated personal data (including tasks, messages, and attachments you authored) are permanently removed from our systems, except where retention is required for legal, accounting, or security purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal information we hold about you through your Profile.
  • Update your name and password at any time.
  • Permanently delete your account and associated data from the Profile screen.
  • Disable push notifications at any time through your device settings. Disabling notifications does not affect your ability to use TaskHub.

Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including hashed passwords, encrypted transport (HTTPS), and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, but we work to protect your data using reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

Children’s Privacy

TaskHub is a business productivity tool and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@vgrand.com.
